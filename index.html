From Curiosity to Intelligence.

Bridging foundational computer science, machine learning models, and real-world production systems.

"I turn data into intelligence and ideas into useful software. Exploring machine learning, deep learning, and intelligent applications through experimentation, engineering, and real-world projects."

My journey began in Computer Engineering, where fundamental questions about how machines process logic quickly evolved into a deeper fascination: how can software learn from patterns, adapt to uncertainty, and make intelligent decisions?

Starting with core programming in C, Java, and Python, I quickly transitioned into Data Science to understand the underlying mathematics of variance, probability, and distribution. Data was no longer just rows in a database — it became the raw signal of real-world dynamics.

From exploratory data analysis and feature engineering, I advanced into Machine Learning: implementing predictive models with Scikit-learn and XGBoost, calibrating probabilities, and analyzing feature attribution with SHAP. Moving deeper into neural architectures, I delved into Deep Learning and Natural Language Processing using TensorFlow and Keras — designing Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) cells and Bidirectional GRUs (BiGRU) that process sequential human language.

Crucially, modeling is only half the craft. The true test of intelligence is real-world deployment. Whether engineering FastAPI microservices, building interactive Plotly/Dash dashboards, or developing industrial quality-control and IoT telemetry systems during internship practice, I focus on turning experimental intelligence into reliable, production-ready software.
